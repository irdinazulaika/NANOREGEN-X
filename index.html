The problem

Nerves can regrow. They just get lost.

Past a 3 cm gap, axons lose their way. Today's fix borrows a nerve from another part of the same patient.

1 in 1,000people injured every year
< 50%regain full function after a nerve graft
2 woundsgraft surgery leaves a numb donor site

Today: a hollow tube

No guidance, no signal path, no biochemical cue.

Ours: an active guide

Fibers to grow along, graphene to carry the signal, peptide to keep cells alive.

The scaffold

Three cues. One construct. No growth factor.

Aligned PCL fibers

Electrospun, micron-scale, lined up like the native nerve. Cells follow the direction. Biodegradable.

Graphene nanoplatelets

Low loading makes the scaffold conductive, so the body's own signals travel along the guide.

Patented bioactive peptide

Bonded to the fiber surface. Mimics laminin and nerve growth factor. Validated in vivo. Cheap and stable.
